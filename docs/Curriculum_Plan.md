# Curriculum Plan — formations woven into lessons

Status: **draft for owner approval** (November 2026). Nothing below beyond Lesson 2.1 is built yet.

## How every lesson works

1. **The idea.** One principle, named in plain English ("Beating a press: draw it, then break it").
2. **The shapes.** Both teams' formations, each shown *without the ball* and *with the ball*, and what the shape change means for this lesson. Formations are taught here, inside lessons, not in a separate section.
3. **The mission.** A 2–3 item checklist the board verifies live from the positions on the pitch.
4. **Plays.** The lesson's situations. Today one hand-built picture; later the generator makes fresh ones from real match moments (goal kick under pressure, striker wins the ball alone, loose ball in midfield, throw-in in your own third).
5. **Debrief.** Each checklist item: what happened, why it matters, a coaching note, and the best sequence the engine found.

Formations are layered: each level introduces new shapes, and later levels keep using the earlier ones.

## Accuracy

- Shapes are representative coaching models, labelled as such in the app.
- Historical associations (Chapman's WM, Brazil 1958's 4-2-4, Cruyff and Bielsa's 3-3-1-3, Ancelotti's Christmas tree) are the commonly cited ones; sources to be added before publishing those lessons.
